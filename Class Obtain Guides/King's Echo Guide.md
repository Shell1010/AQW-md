This class is pretty simple to obtain, can be done within 3-4 hours with prerequisites completed. 6 hours without. To start farming for this class you will need:
- Level 80
- Completion of the Rumbling of Cold Thunder saga
- Rank 10 in the Good and Swordhaven factions
- Alden's Liberation Armor
- [**”The Storm Queen”**](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/felix-taylor-s-quests#CThunder)

Questline begins at [/Terminatemple](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/termina-temple) and you should find Victoria towards the right.

- **”Familial Blessing”**
	- Click on Tara on screen 1

- **”Echo of the King”**
	- Requires you to have each of the items below. You can go to victoria for a quest for each of these items.
	- [Alteon's Reforged Armor](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/alteon-s-reforged-armor)
	- [King Alteon's Crown](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/king-alteon-s-crown)
	- [Royal Dragon Sword](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/royal-dragon-sword)
	- [Alden's Mace](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/alden-s-mace)

--- 
- **”The Path to the King”** - Sword Quest at /portal
	- [Endured Against the Defiler](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/endured-against-the-defiler) 100x
		- From fighting the Destructive Defiler at [/Infernalarena](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/infernal-arena), drops 1x per kill
		- If you’re mem, fighting Graveclaw at [/gravechallenge](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/grave-challenge) will drop 10x per kill
	- [Endured Against the Monster King](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/endured-against-the-monster-king) 90x
		- Fight dethrix at [/ebondungeon](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ebonslate-dungeon)
	- [Endured Against a Fallen Friend](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/endured-against-a-fallen-friend) 100x
		- Fight Ultrasepulchure at [/sepulchurebattle](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sepulchure-battle)
	- [Endured Against Chaos](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/endured-against-chaos) 113x
		- Fight Ultra Chaos Warlord at [/chaosboss](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaos-boss)
	- [Endured Against the Great Dragon](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/endured-against-the-great-dragon) 70x
		- Fight against big dragon at end at [/naoisegrave](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/naoise-s-grave) 

---
- **”Purify the Crown”** - Crown Quest at /cleric
	- [Trace of Chaos](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/trace-of-chaos) 13x
		- Fight drakath at [/ultradrakath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-drakath) for it, low drop rate
		- Merge for it at [/championdrakath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/champion-drakath-location) using 5x Drakath Insignia
	- [Whispers of Chaos](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/whispers-of-chaos) 40x
		- Fight Ultra Dreadhaven General at [/ultradread](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-dreadfight)
			- Use Chaos Slayer, buffed against chaos monsters and pretty good
		- Alternatively, fight Slugwrath at [/dreadhaven](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dreadhaven)
		- [Calcified Tear](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/calcified-tear) 60x
			- Fight the QoM at [/transformation](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/transformation) for a chance to get the tears
			- Alternatively fight at [/queenbattle](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/queen-s-battle) for a 100% drop

---
- **”The King’s Armour”**
	- [Gold Voucher 500k](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/gold-voucher-500k) 100x
		- Yes 50m Gold, farm at /sevencircleswar if f2p
		- Otherwise farm at /lichwar if mem
		- Or Leery
	- [Dwarven Gold](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dwarven-gold) 30x
		- Farm this from dwarves at [/thelimacity](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/thelima-city)
	- Atlas Gold 75x (currently no wiki page)
		- Head to [/atlasfalls](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/atlas-falls) and bully King zedek for a guaranteed drop
		- Otherwise just defeat his lions if it’s difficult
	- Skye Gold 50x
		- Head to [/queeniona](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/queen-iona-challenge-fight) and kill her 25 times
		- Pretty sure one of the cold thunder maps you can farm for this, just that since wiki didn’t update it yet I cannot confirm locations etc. I only did the iona challenge fight.
	- Camlan Gold 120x
		- Defeat the two elve minibosses near the end at [/camlan](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/camlan-forest)
