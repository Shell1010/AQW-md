Martial Artist has some pretty simple prerequisites, just be above Level 65 and complete the questline associated with [/hakuvillage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/haku-village), then you can start the questline. It begins at [/hakuvillage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/haku-village) from The Master NPC. Quests look misleadingly difficult but they provide you with shortcuts. Lot of players have reading issue and seem to think you have to farm all 10 million mobs by hand lel.

---
**”Join my Dojo”**
- Self explanatory

**”500 Punches and 500 Kicks”**
- This is the only part of the questline you kinda have to do 1 by 1.
- Kill frogzards at [/nexus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/nexus)
- Kill Gorillaphants at [/arcangrove](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/arcangrove)
- Kill Dragons at [/etherwardes](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/etherstorm-war-desoloth)

---
**”Deathly Slow Start”** and the rest of these quests

- This is the first shortcut quest provided to you by Death at /death
- You are required to complete this entire questline for this to be visible
- This is also the only relevant shortcut quest, as all the others are slower than this method
- Death will provide you with the quest **”Soul Hunter”**, use it to meet all the requirements
	- Join [/dreadfight](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dreadfight) and beat up the Super Dreadhaven General
		- This needs to be done 400x (f2p) or 200x (mem)
	- Join [/hakuwar](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/haku-war) and beat the Zakhvatchik boss
		- This needs to be done 400x (f2p) or 200x (mem)
	- Join [/towerofdoom5](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/tower-of-doom-5) and defeat the Creel
		- This needs to be done 400x (f2p) or 200x (mem)

You basically spam these quests till you hit the 10 million requirement, relatively easy farm, on release we did it in like 2 hours, though now I’m guessing closer to 4-6 hours.
