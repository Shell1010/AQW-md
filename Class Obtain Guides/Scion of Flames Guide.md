Pretty good support class that can kinda substitute LR if you don’t have it and better in some DoT specific comps. Generally a pretty good class overall. Requirements is that you complete the Flame of the Beyond saga, have Pyromancer too. Quest begins at /rubedopeak.

**”Scion of Flames Class”**
- Main Quest Requirements
	- [Aleister the Dawnseeker](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/aleister-the-dawnseeker)
		- From completing the quest at /magnumopus. Beat up the big bad boss for [Flame of the Magnum Opus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-of-the-magnum-opus) 20x
	- [Flame of Albedo (Armor)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-of-albedo-armor)
		- Complete **”Candidus Sol”** at [/forgealbedo](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/forge-albedo) for [White Flame of Albedo](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/white-flame-of-albedo) 150x
	- [Flame of Citrinitas (Armor)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-of-citrinitas-armor)
		- Complete **”Faith Without Hard Work”** at [/fortluma](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/fort-luma) for [Yellow Flame of Citrinitas](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/yellow-flame-of-citrinitas) 100x
	- [Flame of Maleno (Armor)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-of-maleno-armor)
		- Complete **”Argyria”** at [/mountmaleno](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/mount-maleno) for [Black Flame of Maleno](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/black-flame-of-maleno) 75x
	- [Flame of Rubedo (Armor)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-of-rubedo-armor)
		- Complete **”Fórnarkálfur”** at [Warwick Forest](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/warwick-forest) for [Red Flame of Rubedo](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/red-flame-of-rubedo) 100x
- They also require you to have a few other materials
	- [Yew Ember](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/yew-ember) 80x - Drops from a bunch of mobs in FotB saga, anything with high HP = higher drop rate
	- [Steel Ingot](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/steel-ingot) 50x - From [/thelima](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/thelima-city) doing **”Iron Blooded”** quest
	- [Aiwass Diamond](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/aiwass-diamond) 50x - From **”Al Vel Legis”** quest
	- [Gold Voucher 100k](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/gold-voucher-100k) 75x - [[Gold Guide]] about 7.5m Gold so pretty easy

You can also skip requirements by doing the [/flameusurper](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-usurper) ultra. He drops [Great Flame of Yew](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/great-flame-of-yew) which can be used to merge for the armours. You need to kill it 5x for 1 armour.
