Kathool Adept is another birthday class, that is to be released at the time of writing this guide.

Requirements to start:
- Level 80
- Completion of the Voice in the Sea saga
- Rank 10 in the Fishing and Somnia factions (check [[Faction Reputation Guides]] for farming methods)

The class is obtained in two quests:
1. Requires all 8 component armors minus the daily part
2. **Adeptus Kathooli** - Requires the Adeptus Kathooli armor (includes dailies)

---

### **"Kathool Adept"**

You need the following 8 armors to merge for **Adeptus Kathooli** at Kathool's Chest in [/godofdepthsbonus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/god-of-the-depths-bonus-zone):

- Ashray Villager
- Undine Defence Director
- Evacuation Protocol Suit
- Ashray Elf Warden
- Elven Heritage Guardian
- DeepWater Drow
- Midnight Glaucus Sage
- Kathool Acolyte

Plus the merge materials:
- Remnant of the Deep x7
- Adeptus Relic x7

### **Adeptus Kathooli Merge Materials**

**Remnant of the Deep x7**
- Dropped by God of the Depths at [/godofdepthsbonus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/god-of-the-depths-bonus-zone)

**Adeptus Relic x7**
- **Daily Quest: "Frenzy Feast"** at [/godofdepthsbonus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/god-of-the-depths-bonus-zone) from Holothurian
- Stacks to 50, so you only need 7 days of dailies

### **"Adeptus Kathooli"**

Once you have all 8 armors and the merge materials, merge for **Adeptus Kathooli** at Kathool's Chest in [/godofdepthsbonus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/god-of-the-depths-bonus-zone).

Then accept the quest **"Adeptus Kathooli"** from the same shop/area to unlock the class.

---

### **Summary of Gold Vouchers Needed**

Each armor requires Gold Voucher 25k x4. With 8 armors + 3 intermediate Glaucus merges = 11 merges × 4 = **44 Gold Voucher 25k (1,100,000 Gold total)**
