Required to access a decent chunk of the game, often seen as one of the more worthwhile purchases if you’re not a super whale.

- Acquire 1,200 ACs
	- Wait for AQW's Birthday in October or Honorable Hero badges in December and January. Often Times they will give out free ACs.
	- Secondary: Purchase the ACs. This one is self explanatory

- Collect 200 Combat Trophies
	- Join bludrutbrawl and farm 200 Combat Trophies. The best way of completing this easily is by farming the Mithril PvP Amulet +15000, and using it with Healer.
		- To farm the Mithril PvP Amulet +15000, kill the Reaper in [/thevoid](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/the-void) for Platinum and Diamond PVP amulets. Then merge.
	- It’s recommend to farm these materials BEFORE buying the armour

- Head to /shadowfall and find Dage the Evil 
	- Buy Undead Warrior (1,200 ACs)
	- Complete quests: Undead Champion Initiation, Mourn the Soldiers, Understanding Undead Champions, Player vs Power, and Fail to the King.
	- Sell the Undead Warrior armor for 1,080 ACs

**”Undead Champion Initiation”**
- Beat up black knight at [/greenguardwest](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/greenguard-west)

**”Mourn the Soldiers”**
- Skeletal Soldier at [/swordhavenundead](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/swordhaven-undead)
- Chaos Drow at [/dwarfhold](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dwarfhold-keep)
- Fishman Soldier at [/pirates](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/lolosia)
- Dwakel Soldier at [/willowcreek](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/willow-creek)

**”Understanding Undead Champions”**
- Beat up Undead Champion in [/battleunderb](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/battle-under-b)

**”Player vs Power”**
- Use the pre-farmed combat trophies 

**”Fail to the King”**
- Kill Alteon’s knights at [/prison](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/prison)

You should now be a legion member wooooooooo
