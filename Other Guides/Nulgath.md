We’ll split this guide into F2P, ACs, Mem and Whale since I believe there’s a lot of variation between each area.

## F2P

Average F2P player experience is usually just spamming **”Supplies to spin the wheel of chance”** and **”Swindle’s Return Policy”**. The most well known method is heading to [/Ultraalteon](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-alteon-location) and beating up a retired old man for relics to turn in. Also remember to do your void dailies from [/originul](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/memory-of-nulgath-s-quests) which are important for easy nulgath mats.

You can obtain the following
- [Dark Crystal Shard](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-crystal-shard)
- [Tainted Gem](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/tainted-gem)
- [Gem of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/gem-of-nulgath)
- [Essence of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/essence-of-nulgath)
- [Diamond of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/diamond-of-nulgath)
- [Uni 13’s](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/the-contract-of-nulgath)
- [Bloodgems](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/blood-gem-of-the-archfiend) (Return Policy)
- [Totem of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/totem-of-nulgath) (Using [Swindle’s Receipt](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/receipt-of-swindle) at [Swindle’s Ripoff Emporium](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/swindle-s-ripoff-emporium))

If you’re stacked on Gold you can probably use **”The Assistant”** too if you can’t be bothered to kill things.

You can also use [Drudgen the Assistant’s quests](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/drudgen-the-assistant-s-quests) (specifically **”Drudgen the Salesman”**). On Artix you can join yulgar-9889 to grab the quest, or just have a friend (impossible) who has the pet.

**Extra Note:**
You can also fight the Level 90 Hydras at [/hydrachallenge](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/hydra-challenge) for relics. Ultra Alteon is approximately a 50% drop rate for Relics, whereas Hydras are at 25-30% drop rate. Doing my own tests I can sustain 8 Ultra Alteon (has a long respawn timer), which equates to about 4 relics a minute.

You only need 14 KPM on Hydras (very doable with the newly buffed Chaos Slayer) to match Ultra Alteon, and anything higher beats it. Hydras gooder

## ACs

You mostly do the same things as the previous except that now you have a few more quests to help you.

**[Crag and Bamboozle](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/crag-bamboozle)** costs 5k ACs, provides:
- [Relic of Chaos](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/relic-of-chaos) (**”Bamboozle vs Drudgen”**)
- [Uni 13’s](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/the-contract-of-nulgath) (**”Diamond Exchange"**)
- [Diamond of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/diamond-of-nulgath) (**”GRUMBLE, GRUMBLE…”** and **”Crag’s Thirst”**)

[Nulgath’s Birthday Gift](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/nulgath-s-birthday-gift-s-quest) costs 1.5k ACs, provides:
- [Basically everything but I think rates are worse than just using Crag or Drudgen?](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/nulgath-s-birthday-gift-s-quest)

[Drudgen the Assistant](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/drudgen-the-assistant-s-quests) costs 5k ACs, provides:
- i dont use this nor own it, looks mid ngl

[Swindle’s Hut](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/swindle-bilk-s-to-go-hut) costs 2k ACs, provides:
- Similar quest to return policy (**”Swindle’s Bonus Deal”**)
- [Bloodgems](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/blood-gem-of-the-archfiend) (**”Bloody Chaos”**)
