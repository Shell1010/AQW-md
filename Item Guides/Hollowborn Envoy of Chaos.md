Badges we love yes. Must be level 75 to start this questline.


**”Stirring Discord”**
- [Chaotic Draconian Wings](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaotic-draconian-wings)
	- Get it from [/dwarfhold](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dwarfhold-keep) by killing Chaotic Draconian
- [Shard of Chaos](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shard-of-chaos) 100x
	- Kill gemeralds at [/blindingsnow](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/northlands-forest) for the easiest time
- [Chaotic Knight Helm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaotic-knight-helm)
	- Buy at [/crownsreach](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/crownsreach)
- [Chaotic War Essence](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaotic-war-essence) 15x
	- From [/chaosboss](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaos-boss) by killing the Ultra Chaos Warlord
- [Chaos Pirate Crew](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaos-pirate-crew)
	- Kill Heart of Chaos at [/lagunabeach](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/laguna-beach)
- [Maledictus Magum](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/maledictus-magum)
	- Go to [/backroom](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/back-room) and kill the Book Wyrm for it
