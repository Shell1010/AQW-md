Big sword

**Requirements:**
- Level 95
- Must have completed "Power of the Undead Legion" quest chain (check the Legion Revenant guide for this)
- Must have the following items in inventory to accept the quest:
	- Underworld Blade of DOOM
	- [[Necrotic Sword of Doom]]

---

### **Prerequisite Items**

#### **Underworld Blade of DOOM**
**Location:** [/dagechallenge](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-challenge)
- Dropped by Dage the Evil (Monster) (Level 80) at [/dagechallenge](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-challenge)


---

### **"Necrotic Sword of the Underworld" Quest**

**Location:** [/ultradage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-dage) from Calamitus
**Requirements:** Level 95, must have the 2 prerequisite items above in inventory

**Items Required:**
- Legion Token x25,000
- Beast Soul x25
- Soul Sand x7
- Dage the Evil Insignia x5

**Rewards:**
- 1,000,000 Gold
- 1,000,000 Exp
- Necrotic Blade of the Underworld

---

### **Material Breakdown**

#### **Legion Token x25,000**
- **Quest: "First Class Entertainment"** at [/legionarena](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/axeros-quests) - best source if you have Shogun Paragon Pet, otherwise use pet
- Alternatively shogun paragon pet.
- **Stacks to 50,000**

#### **Beast Soul x25**
**Location:** [/sevencircleswar](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/seven-circles-war)
- Dropped by The Beast (Monster)
- **Stacks to 300**

#### **Soul Sand x7**
**Locations:** [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/underworld), [/dagehouse](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-s-house), [/darkbirthday](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-birthday), [/legioncastle](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-castle)

**Quest Rewards:**
- **Quest: "Another One Bites the Dust!"** at [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/vulcar-s-quest) from Vulcar
- **Quest: "Daily Exercise 5: God of the Depths"** at [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-the-evil-s-champion-quests)
- **Quest: "Daily Exercise 6: Dage the Dark Lord"** at [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-the-evil-s-champion-quests)
- **Stacks to 50**

#### **Dage the Evil Insignia x5**
- **Weekly Quest: "Dage, Lord of the Undead Legion"** at [/ultradage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-dage) from Calamitus
	- Defeat Dage the Dark Lord
	- Rewards 5 Insignias per week
	- **Takes 1 week** for 5 Insignias

This is significantly easier than Sin of the Underworld since it doesn't require Sin of the Undying, PvP, or the 6 additional weapon components. A good stepping stone if you're working towards the full Sin of the Underworld.
