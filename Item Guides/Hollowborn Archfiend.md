Nulgath makes everyone else do the work and you get to do it for Lae instead. Must be level 95 to finish this questline, and you need [Lae's Hardcore Contract](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/lae-s-hardcore-contract) before anything else.

---

**”In the Fiend's Shadow”**

- Requirements
    - Must be Level 85
    - Must have [Lae's Hardcore Contract](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/lae-s-hardcore-contract) in your inventory
- [Phoenix Blade](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/phoenix-blade)
    - Drops from Red Dragon or Onyx Lava Dragon
    - Alternatively merge it from [Phoenix Blades](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/phoenix-blades) + 50k Gold
- [Chaotic Tentacles (1)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chaotic-tentacles-1)
    - Kill Chaos Lord Lionfang at [/stormtemple](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/the-storm-temple)
- [Totem of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/totem-of-nulgath)
    - Use [Swindle's Receipt](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/receipt-of-swindle) at [Swindle's Ripoff Emporium](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/swindle-s-ripoff-emporium)
- [Hollow Soul (Misc)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/hollow-soul-misc) 50x
    - From [/shadowrealm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-realm) hollowborn sentinels

This is the only quest that gives you [Void Soul](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/void-soul) in any meaningful amount (10x per turn in), and you are going to need 150 of them in total. We will get to the maths later, just know you will be doing this one a lot.

You also get to pick a Hollowborn Void weapon or helm, take whichever you like.

        - [Crimson Plate of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/crimson-plate-of-nulgath)

        - [Ungodly Reavers of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ungodly-reavers-of-nulgath)

        - [Overfiend Blade of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/overfiend-blade-of-nulgath)
        - [Dark Makai of Nulgath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-makai-of-nulgath)
- [Blighted Zubami Badge](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/blighted-zubami-badge)
    - Beat up the blighted dragon at /deleuzetundra
- [Spoil of Salek](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/spoil-of-salek)

The annoying part is the 7 Nulgath items you need in your inventory, so if you have been doing Nulgath stuff for a while you probably have most of these already. Refer to the [Nulgath Guide](https://www.aqwhub.com/other-guides/nulgath/) for the mats.

The reward is a Hollowborn version of one of the Nulgath weapons, with a random chance at a Hollowborn Makai pet.

**Void Soul maths**

You need 150 Void Souls in total (50 for **”Muddled Past”**, 100 for **”Tug and Pull”**) and they only come from two quests:

- **”In the Fiend's Shadow”** gives 10x
- **”Fiend's Purgatory”** gives 1x

Since **”Fiend's Purgatory”** is a pain to do, the easiest way is to do it once and spam **”In the Fiend's Shadow”** for the rest. The order that works out perfectly is:

1. Do **”In the Fiend's Shadow”** 5 times (50 Void Souls)
2. Do **”Fiend's Purgatory”** once (51 Void Souls)
3. Do **”Muddled Past”** (1 Void Soul left)
4. Do **”In the Fiend's Shadow”** 10 more times (101 Void Souls)
5. Do **”Tug and Pull”**

So **”In the Fiend's Shadow”** is done 15 times in total.
