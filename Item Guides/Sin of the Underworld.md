Another big sword. 

**”Sin of the Underworld”** is from Calamitus in [/ultradage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-dage) and requires [Sin of the Undying](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sin-of-the-undying) in your inventory to accept. 

---

**”Undying Fealty”**

Gets you [Sin of the Undying](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sin-of-the-undying), click the sword on Screen 5 of [/atlasfalls](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/atlas-falls) to start the quest.

- Requirements
    - Must have completed **”Dante to Beatrice”** from [Misaru's Quests](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/misaru-s-quests#Atlas)
    - Must be Level 80
- [Legion Token](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-token) 5000x
- [Broken Chain](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/broken-chain) 150x
    - From /atlaspromenade
- [Atlas Lion Pelt](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/atlas-lion-pelt) 20x
    - From /atlaskingdom
- [Atlas Regalia](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/atlas-regalia) 20x
    - From [/atlasfalls](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/atlas-falls)
- [Beast Soul](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/beast-soul) 20x
    - From /sevencircleswar
- [Essence of Wrath](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/essence-of-wrath) 20x
    - From /sevencircleswar
- [Essence of Violence](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/essence-of-violence) 20x
    - From /sevencircleswar
- [Essence of Treachery](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/essence-of-treachery) 20x
    - From /sevencircleswar

**”Sin of the Underworld”**

- Requirements
    - Must be Level 95
    - Must have [Sin of the Undying](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sin-of-the-undying) in your inventory
    - Must have completed **”Power of the Undead Legion”**
- [Legion Token](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-token) 25000x
    - Do first class entertainment at [/legionarena](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-arena)
    - Otherwise with Shogun do spring cleaning at /fotia
    - Also do your Legion dailies from Dage, see the Soul Sand section below
- [Undead Champion Blade](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/undead-champion-blade)
    - Random reward from **”Legion Exercise Number 1”**, see below
- [Corrupted Dragon Slayer](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/corrupted-dragon-slayer)
    - Random reward from **”Legion Exercise Number 4”**, see below
- [Wrath of the Legion Blade](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/wrath-of-the-legion-blade)
    - Underworld Team Shop in [/legionpvp](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-pvp)
    - Merge [Legion Combat Trophy](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-combat-trophy) 300x
- [Fatal Keraunos](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/fatal-keraunos)
    - SoulForge in [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/underworld)
    - Merge [Dage's Favor](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-s-favor) 1500x, [Obsidian Rock](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/obsidian-rock) 333x and [Solidified Soul](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/solidified-soul) 333x
- [Yami no Ronin Katana](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/yami-no-ronin-katana)
    - Reward from **”The Edge of an Era”** in [/darkally](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-ally), see below
- [Dual Legion Soul Devourers](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dual-legion-soul-devourers)
    - Vulcar's Merge in [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/underworld)
    - Merge [Legion Soul Devourer](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-soul-devourer) 1x and [Soul Essence](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/soul-essence) 10x
- [Dage the Evil Insignia](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-the-evil-insignia) 30x
    - Weekly quest **”Dage, Lord of the Undead Legion”** from Calamitus in [/ultradage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-dage)
    - Beat up Dage the Dark Lord for 5 Insignia per week, so this takes 6 weeks minimum
    - Start this first, it is the only part you cannot speed up

The reward is the sword and 1m Gold and 1m EXP. The sword does 51% more damage to all monsters and gives 30% more gold/exp/rep/CP.

**Legion Exercise Number 1 and 4**

Both of these are from Dage in [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/underworld) (also 8-Bit Battleon Town and Dage's House) and need [Undead Champion (Armor)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/undead-champion-armor) in your inventory. The sword is a **random** reward, so you will be doing these a few times. You get 25 Legion Tokens per turn in, so it is not a complete waste.

**Exercise 1** gives either Undead Champion Blade or Legendary Golden Death Blade

- [Flawless DracoHorn](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flawless-dracohorn) 40x
- Golden DracoHeart
    - Kill Golden Draconian
- Dark DracoHeart
    - Kill Dark Draconian
- Mammoth DracoHeart
    - Kill Bronze Draconian
- Water DracoHeart
    - Kill Water Draconian
- Venom DracoHeart
    - Kill Venom Draconian
- Iron DracoHeart
    - Kill Purple Draconian

**Exercise 4** gives Corrupted Dragon Slayer, Judgement Scythe, PainSaw of Eidolon or a Soul Eater

- [Soul Scythe](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/soul-scythe)
- Frostbit Skull 15x
    - Kill Skeletal Ice Mage
- Potent Viper's Blood
    - Kill Lesser Shadow Serpent

**Yami no Ronin Katana**

The one that takes the longest, it is a chain from Dage in [/darkally](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-ally). You need to have completed **”Defeat the Underfiend”** first, which is the end of the Dark Ally quests (all pretty easy kill quests).

Then from **”The Path to Power”** chain, do these in order:

- **”The Path to Power”**
    - [Sapphire Orb](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sapphire-orb) 13x
        - Beat up Ultra Aeacus in /judgement and Legion Lich Lord in /frozenlair
    - [Legion Token](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-token) 17500x
    - [Meditation](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/meditation)
        - Turn in a Bone Sigil from /legionarena if you own an old BladeMaster/SwordMaster class
    - [Aeacus Empowered](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/aeacus-empowered) 50x
- **”Embracing Darkness”**
    - [Dark Wisp](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-wisp) 444x
- **”Forge Your Steel, Steel Your Resolve”**
    - [Flame-Forged Metal](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/flame-forged-metal) 13x
    - [Obsidian Rock](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/obsidian-rock) 108x
    - [Eternity Flame](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/eternity-flame)
    - [Weapon Imprint](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/weapon-imprint) 15x
    - [Shadow Katana Blueprint](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-katana-blueprint)
- **”The Edge of an Era”**
    - [Perfect Orochi Scales](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/perfect-orochi-scales) 888x
        - From Orochi in /shadowfortress or /shogunwar
    - [Shadow Katana Blade](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-katana-blade)
    - [Oni Skull Charm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/oni-skull-charm)
        - From Mitsu Bishi
    - [Darkened Essence](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/darkened-essence) 600x
        - From the warriors in /shadowrealmpast

The wiki also lists a **”Yami no Ronin (Shortcut)”** version of this chain, so have a look at [Dage's Quests](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dage-the-evil-s-quests#Yami) before you start grinding Orochi scales.

**Soul Sand and Soul Essence**

[Soul Essence](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/soul-essence) is needed 10x for the Dual Legion Soul Devourers

- Requires [Legion Bonfire](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-bonfire-s-quest) quest
- Grrberus' Flame x1
    - Dropped by [Grrrberus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/grrrberus)
- [Soul Sand](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/soul-sand) 3x

So that is 10 Flames and 30 Soul Sand. Add the 2 Soul Sand for the [Legion Soul Devourer](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-soul-devourer) (it is also 200k Gold) and you need 32 Soul Sand in total.

For Soul Sand, get [Legion Promotion](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-promotion) and do the dailies from Dage in [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/underworld). They also give Legion Tokens so you are farming two things at once:

- God of the Depths gives 250 Legion Tokens and 1 Soul Sand
- Dage the Dark Lord in [/ultradage](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-dage) gives 250 Legion Tokens and 2 Soul Sand
- Ultra Aeacus, Legion Lich Lord, The Beast and Devastator Deimos give 100 to 150 Legion Tokens each

---

**Solidified Soul and Obsidian Rock**

Do **”Obsidian Mining”** and **”Soul Forgery”** from Dage in [/underworld](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/underworld), you get 1, 3 or 5 per turn in. Or just buy the Solidified Souls from the SoulForge for 2 Legion Tokens and 66 Gold each (666 Tokens and about 22k Gold for 333).
