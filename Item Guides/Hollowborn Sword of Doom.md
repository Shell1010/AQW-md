Must be Level 65, have Rank 10 Evil and must have [Lae's Hardcore Contract](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/lae-s-hardcore-contract) in your inventory. The final part of the grind requires member.

The hollowborn doomblade part of this quest is for RGOW too, this doesn’t require member.

**”A Dark Knight”**
- [Empowered Essence](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/empowered-essence) 10x
	- Drops from enemies in [/shadowrealmpast](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-realm-past)
- [Shadowworn](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadowworn)
	- Drops from Shadow Lord in [/shadowrealmpast](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-realm-past)
	- Make sure you grab [Shadow DoomReaver](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-doomreaver) too as you’ll need it for the next quest
- [Shadowscythe Venom Head](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadowscythe-venom-head) 
	- Buy from evil rep shop in [/shadowfall](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadowfall)
- [Hollow Soul](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/hollow-soul-misc) 10x
	- From [/shadowrealm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-realm) hollowborn sentinels

This rewards you with Dark Fragments, you will need 125x Dark Fragments for the badge. So you may have to do this a few times.

**”A Dark Knight Rises”**
- [Doomatter](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doomatter) 10x
	- I recommend using Swindle Receipts at [/tercessuinotlim](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/tercessuinotlim) for this as it’s the easiest
	- Otherwise you’ll just have to kill the Creature Creation in [/maul](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/chopping-maul)
	- For mem, kill Fear Feeder in [/creepy](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/fear-war-location)
- [Shadow DoomReaver](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-doomreaver)
	- Drops from Shadow Lord in [/shadowrealmpast](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-realm-past)
- [Worshipper of Doom](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/worshipper-of-doom)
	- Drops from Corrupted Luma in [/lumafortress](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/luma-fortress) 
	- RNG kinda ass iirc, have fun lmao
- [Ingredients?](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ingredients) 10x
	- Dropped by killing binky at [/doomvault](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doom-vault)
	- Alternatively kill [/ultracarnax](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/ultra-carnax-location) for mem
- [Dark Fragment](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-fragment) 5x
	- Previous quest reward

This rewards you with Doom Fragments, you will need to complete this quest 5x times for the badge.

**”A Dark Knight Falls”**
- [Uni 25](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/essence-of-the-shadow-void)
	- I recommend just shilling out 3.75m Gold at [/tercessuinotlim](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/tercessuinotlim) swindle merge
- [(Necro) Scroll of Dark Arts](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/necro-scroll-of-dark-arts)
	- Beat up vordred at [/epicvordred](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/epic-vordred)
	- Low drop rate lel
	-  Or alternatively doing the daily VA quests from [Braeus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/braeus-quests) can eventually drop it
- [Royal ShadowScythe Blade](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/royal-shadowscythe-blade)
	- Buy from Gravelyn at [/shadowfall](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadowfall) for 1m Gold
- [Weapon Imprint](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/weapon-imprint) 
	- Beat up Raxgore at [/doomvaultb](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doom-vault-b)
- [Dark Fragment](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-fragment) 20x
	- First quest reward
- [Doom Fragment](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doom-fragment) 5x
	- Second quest reward

Grab the hollowborn doomblade for RGOW if you haven’t got it already.

**”A Dark Knight Returns”**

- Must have the following items in your inventory:
    - [Hollowborn Shadow of Fate](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/hollowborn-shadow-of-fate)
    - [Lae's Hardcore Contract](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/lae-s-hardcore-contract)
    - [Necrotic Sword of Doom (Sword)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/necrotic-sword-of-doom-sword)
    - [Sepulchure's DoomKnight Armor](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sepulchure-s-doomknight-armor)
    - [Sepulchure's Original Helm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sepulchure-s-original-helm)

- [Dark Energy (Misc)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-energy-misc) 10000x
	- Fight the Evolved Overlord at [/necrocavern](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/necropolis-cavern) drops 1-100x per kill
	- Doom Overlord at [/necrodungeon](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/necropolis-dungeon) also does the same
- [(Necro) Scroll of Dark Arts](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/necro-scroll-of-dark-arts) 3x
	- Beat up vordred at [/epicvordred](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/epic-vordred)
	- Low drop rate lel
	-  Or alternatively doing the daily VA quests from [Braeus](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/braeus-quests) can eventually drop it
- [Bones from the Void Realm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/bones-from-the-void-realm)
	- [Void Aura](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/void-aura) 50x
	- [Bone Dust](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/bone-dust) 50x
	- Merge at [/shadowfall](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadowfall)
- [Doom Heart](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doom-heart)
	- From killing seppy at [/sepulchurebattle](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/sepulchure-battle)
	- Low Drop rate lel
- [Weapon Imprint](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/weapon-imprint) 12x
	- Beat up Raxgore at [/doomvaultb](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doom-vault-b)
- [Desolich's Dark Horn](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/desolich-s-dark-horn) 3z
	- Beat up Desolich at [/desolich](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/desolich-location)
- [Dark Fragment](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/dark-fragment) 30x
	- First quest reward
- [Doom Fragment](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/doom-fragment) 10x
	- Second quest reward
