Aranx finally gives you his wings, but it is going to cost you 17.5 million Gold first. Must be level 90, and you also get the **Celestial of Mysteries Character Page Badge** for finishing the quest.

**Requirements:**

- Must have completed **”Filial Impiety”** from [Azalith's Quests](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/azalith-s-quests)
- Level 90
- [Archangel of Mysteries](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/archangel-of-mysteries), [Archangel of Mysteries Helm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/archangel-of-mysteries-helm) and [Archangel of Mysteries Wings](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/archangel-of-mysteries-wings) in inventory

**Infernalis Penna and Infernalis Oculus**

Both are rewards from quests at [/championazalith](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/champion-azalith), 1 per turn in.

- **”Head Hunters”** gives Infernalis Penna
    - [Maah-na's Wing](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/maah-na) 1x, stacks to 100
        - Dropped by Maah-na
    - [Akh-a's Wing](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/akh-a) 1x, stacks to 100
        - Dropped by Akh-a
- **”Azalith's Ambition”** gives Infernalis Oculus
    - Azalith's Eye 1x, stacks to 100
        - Dropped by Azalith (Level 60)

You need 150 Penna and 44 Oculus for all three pieces, so that is 150 runs of **”Head Hunters”** and 44 runs of **”Azalith's Ambition”**.

For the rest of the merge items (Infernal Token, Champion Sash, Celestial Quintessence, Divine Down and Golden Crown) check each item page on the wiki.

**Infernal Arena**

All the arena drops come from fighting the bosses at [/infernalarena](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/infernal-arena). You need to have completed **”FINAL FIGHT!”** in the Celestial Arena first (the one that gives the Celestial Champion badge). Each boss is a quest from Aranx there, just kill them again and again until you have enough.

- **Deadly Duo** gives Duo's Dinner
- **Cervus Malus** gives Cervus Dente
- **Key of Sholemoh** gives Infernal Incantation
- **Azalith's Scythe** gives Scythe Shard
- **Na'al** gives Champion's Seal

Each of those is a one at a time kill for 1 item, so it is 35 kills each and 20 for Na'al. Good luck.

---

**”Celestial Dianoia”**

Gets you [Infernal Down](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/infernal-down), 1 per turn in. This is the only way I could find to get it, so you are doing this 50 times.

- Requirements
    - Must have completed **”Filial Impiety”** from [Azalith's Quests](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/azalith-s-quests)
- Eudae Metanoia 3x
    - Dropped by Eudae in [/infernaldianoia](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/infernal-dianoia)
    - Stacks to 30
- Avatar of Time's Hand 1x
    - Dropped by Avatar of Time
    - Stacks to 10
- Nightstar 1x
    - Dropped by Aranx, Nightstar
    - Stacks to 10

For 50 Infernal Down that is 150 Eudae Metanoia, 50 Avatar of Time's Hand and 50 Nightstar. The stack limits mean you can only do 10 runs at a time, so turn in 10, farm again, and repeat 5 times.
