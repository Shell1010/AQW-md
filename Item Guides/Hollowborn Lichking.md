ANOTHER SHINY BADGE WOOOOOOOOOOOO

**”Draftless”**
- [Legion Token](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-token) 100x
	- Do first class entertainment at [/legionarena](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-arena)
	- Otherwise with Shogun do spring cleaning at /fotia
- [Undead Energy](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/undead-energy) 50x
	- Kill Doom Overlord at [/necrodungeon](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/necropolis-dungeon) 
- [Hollow Soul (Misc)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/hollow-soul-misc) 25x
	- From [/shadowrealm](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/shadow-realm) hollowborn sentinels

For the shiny badge you will need to this quest 168x for the Soul Fragments, but considering these materials are often done in bulk, this is probably not very hard to do.

So total materials:
- [Legion Token](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/legion-token) 16800x
- [Undead Energy](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/undead-energy) 8400x
- [Hollow Soul (Misc)](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/hollow-soul-misc) 4200x
