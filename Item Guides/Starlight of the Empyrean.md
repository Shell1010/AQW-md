Another Destiny axe, except this one wants you to farm a dragon every single day for three weeks.

This is a merge at **Nova Shrine Gifts** in [/astralshrine](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/astral-shrine) or [/novashrine](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/nova-shrine), and it does 51% more damage to all monsters.

### **Star Piece**

**Location:** [/starfield](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/starfield)

- Dropped by every monster in [/starfield](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/starfield)
- Stacks to 10,000

You need 350 in total, 250 for the Empyrean and 100 for the Star Light of Destiny. Just kill anything in the map.

---

### **Star of the Empyrean**

This is the slow part. It is a reward from **”Asterism's Toll”** and you can only turn it in **once per day**, so 21 Stars means 21 days minimum.

- Requirements
    - Must be Level 95
    - Must have completed **”Hoshiyoru”**, see below
- Astral's Supernova
    - Beat the Astral Empyrean in [/astralshrine](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/astral-shrine)

The quest is from Empress Ai No Miko in [/astralshrine](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/astral-shrine) and gives 350k Gold, 1m EXP and 1 Star of the Empyrean. Star of the Empyrean stacks to 100, so there is no need to spend them as you go.

---

### **Nova Shrine Quests**

You need to have finished **”Hoshiyoru”** before **”Asterism's Toll”** unlocks. All of these are from Empress Ai No Miko in [/novashrine](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/nova-shrine) and you only need to do them once.

**”Byakko”**

- Tiger Fur 20x
    - Dropped by Armored Tiger or Firestorm Tiger in /fireforge

**”Suzaku”**

- Harpy Feather 50x
    - Dropped by Flaming Harpy in /dreampalace

**”Genbu”**

- Silver Scales 15x
    - Dropped by Quicksilver in /yokaitreasure
- Nagami Scales 15x
    - Dropped by Nagami in /hakuvillage

**”Seiryu”**

- Zmey Fang 15x
    - Dropped by Zmey Warrior in /hakuwar
- Zakhvatchik Fang 1x
    - Dropped by Zakhvatchik in /hakuwar

**”Hoshiyoru”**

- Planetary Halo 1x
    - Beat the Nova Empyrean in [/novashrine](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/nova-shrine)
    - Rewards [Cosmic Stardust](http://aqwwiki.wikidot.com/cosmic-stardust)
