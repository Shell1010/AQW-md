After it’s recent balance change, this is now one of the best classes in the game. With the amount of utility it provides both in raw DPS and support.

## Skills Overview

![Duel](https://aqwwiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/classes-skills/CC1.png)
- **Duel** 
	- 0 Mana Cost
	- 1.75 Seconds Cooldown
	- 55% Physical Damage / 0.55 AP2
	- Medium Range Attack that deals damage to 1 target
	- Advances your turn, damaging your opponent
	- Damage is increased by 75% per stack of Raise the Stakes you have
		- Description states 50% which is a LIE

---

![Draw](https://aqwwiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/classes-skills/CC2.png)
- **Draw**
	- 0 Mana Cost
	- 1.5 Seconds Cooldown
	- Physical
	- Medium Range Status Skill that applies to 1 target
	- Draws a card for you and your opponent, applying one of three random cards
		- These cards do nothing, but can be activated by Playing or Dealing them
	- Drawing again replaces the existing cards

Main skill you spam for cards. Yes.

---

![Play](https://aqwwiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/classes-skills/CC3.png)
- **Play**
	- 0 Mana Cost
	- 9 Seconds Cooldown
	- 150% Physical Damage / 1.5 AP2
	- Medium Range Attack that deals damage to 1 target
	- Activates your opponent's card, applying a debuff to them based on the card, and consuming Raise the Stakes
	- If you have Vigor or your opponent has Weakness, deal extra damage
		- PaladinSlayer: -20% Defense and Haste and prevent your foe from healing for 6s - Green
		- Necromancer: -40% Damage dealt for 12s - Blue
		- DoomKnight: -50% Defense, Crit Chance, and Dodge for 12s - Red

Ideally you search for Reds. Greens/Blues become more relevant during support.

---

![Deal](https://aqwwiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/classes-skills/CC4.png)
- **Deal**
	- 0 Mana Cost
	- 9 Seconds Cooldown
	- True Damage
	- Infinite Range Status Skill that applies to 4 friendly targets
	- Activates your held card, applying buffs to you and your teammates depending on your held card, and consuming Raise the Stakes if Dealt
		- Paladin: +70% All, Phys, and Mag defense with a large HoT for 3s - Blue
		- Bard: +20% Damage, +10% Haste, +5% Crit, and -20% Mana Cast, and a Heal over Time for 18s - Green
		- Legendary Hero: +40% STR, INT, and LCK, +25% Damage, and +40% Crit Damage for 6s - Red
	- If you apply a Shared buff, Raise the Stakes is not consumed.

Ideally you search for Reds. Greens/Blues become more relevant during support.

---

![Card Toss](https://aqwwiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/classes-skills/CC5.png)
- **Card Toss**
	- 0 Mana Cost
	- 1.5 Seconds Cooldown
	- 190% Physical Damage / 1.9 AP2
	- Infinite Range Attack that deals damage to 1 target
	- Toss your unused cards at your opponent, dealing damage
	- Gain a stack of Raise the Stakes, for each card, increasing your skill damage by 75% per stack up to 4 stacks, lasting 10 seconds
		- Description states 50% which is a LIE
	- Raise the Stakes is consumed when you next Play or Deal
		- What actually consumes the aura is the Dealt and Played auras, but not the Shared auras 
		- This is important for optimising it

You spam this for big DPS.
